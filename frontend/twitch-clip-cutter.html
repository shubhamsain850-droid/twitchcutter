Twitch Clip Cutter — FAQs

Answers to the most common questions about downloading and clipping Twitch moments.

How do I download a Twitch clip on my phone (iPhone / Android)? +
Open Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, visit twitchcutter.pages.dev/twitch-clip-cutter, paste your Twitch clip URL, and tap Download. On iOS, tap the download icon in Safari and save to Files or your Photos app.
How do I clip longer than 60 seconds on Twitch? +
Twitch strictly caps native clips at 60 seconds. With TwitchCutter, simply open the streamer's VOD, copy the VOD link, and set your desired in/out timestamps. You can slice clips of 2 minutes, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, or longer with zero restrictions.
Are clips downloaded with audio? +
Yes! All downloaded clips include full stereo high-bitrate game and microphone audio, perfectly synchronized with video. If a portion was muted by Twitch due to DMCA music, that specific section will remain muted in the source.
Can I download deleted Twitch clips? +
If a clip has been completely purged from Twitch's CDN servers by the streamer, it cannot be downloaded. We recommend clipping and downloading memorable moments as soon as they happen.
