Twitch VOD & Clip Cutter — FAQ

Common questions about cutting Twitch VODs, downloading clips, and saving past broadcasts.

How do I download part of a Twitch VOD? +
To download part of a Twitch VOD: (1) Copy the Twitch VOD URL and paste it into TwitchCutter. (2) Click Fetch Stream. (3) Set your start and end timestamps using the slider or type them in. (4) Click Cut & Download Clip. Only that selected portion is sliced and exported as an MP4.
Can I clip longer than 60 seconds on Twitch? +
Yes! Twitch's native clip creator limits clips to a maximum of 60 seconds. TwitchCutter removes this limitation entirely, letting you slice any length from 5 seconds up to 2 hours from any public VOD.
Can I cut a Twitch VOD without downloading the full video? +
Yes, that is TwitchCutter's core superpower. Because 8-hour Twitch VODs can exceed 25–40 GB, downloading the whole file is painful. Our cloud server extracts only the HLS segments matching your start and end times, saving your bandwidth and time.
How do I trim a Twitch stream online? +
TwitchCutter is a free online Twitch stream trimmer. Open TwitchCutter in your browser, enter the broadcast link, use the interactive dual slider to set your in and out points, and download your trimmed clip as an MP4.
How long do Twitch VODs last before getting deleted? +
Twitch VOD expiration periods are: Standard streamers: 7 days. Twitch Affiliates: 14 days. Twitch Partners, Turbo, and Prime members: 60 days. Once expired, Twitch permanently deletes past broadcasts. Use TwitchCutter to save your favorite streams before they disappear!
How do I download a Twitch VOD? +
Paste the Twitch VOD link into TwitchCutter and click Fetch Stream. Set the start timestamp to 00:00:00 and the end timestamp to the full duration of the stream, pick your resolution (1080p, 720p), and click Cut & Download Clip.
Can I download someone else's Twitch VOD? +
Yes, you can download clips or segments from any public Twitch VOD or stream. Note that subscriber-only VODs require channel subscription credentials and are not publicly scrapable.
Do Twitch clips expire like VODs do? +
No, Twitch clips do not automatically expire like VODs do. However, streamers can manually delete clips or delete the source VOD, which can occasionally orphan clips. Saving them as local MP4 files guarantees you never lose them.
Can I download Twitch clips in 1080p 60FPS? +
Yes! TwitchCutter automatically queries all broadcast resolutions provided by Twitch's HLS streams. You can download clips in 1080p60 Full HD, 720p60 HD, 480p, or 360p.
What video format does TwitchCutter export? +
All clips are packaged in universal H.264 MP4 format with AAC audio. This ensures instant compatibility with Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, and CapCut.
Do I need to install FFmpeg, Python, or extensions? +
No. All stream slicing, FFmpeg processing, and packaging happen remotely in our high-speed cloud infrastructure. You just click download.
Does TwitchCutter work on mobile phones? +
Yes! TwitchCutter is designed to be mobile-first and responsive. You can paste a Twitch link in mobile Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android and save MP4 clips straight to your device gallery.
Is TwitchCutter legal to use? +
Yes. Using TwitchCutter to create clips and save moments for personal archiving or fair-use commentary and transformative editing is generally acceptable. Always respect streamers' intellectual property and credit original creators when reposting content.
Why are parts of some Twitch VODs muted? +
Twitch has an automated Audible Magic system that scans streams for copyrighted music and mutes offending audio sections in the VOD. If a section is muted on Twitch, any downloaded clip of that section will also be muted because the source audio was permanently stripped.
Is TwitchCutter affiliated with Twitch.tv or Amazon? +
No. TwitchCutter is an independent open-source web application designed for gaming editors and fans. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Twitch Interactive, Inc. or Amazon.com.
