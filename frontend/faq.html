Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about cutting Twitch streams, downloading VODs, audio muting, and video formats.

📼 Twitch VODs & Stream Archiving

1. How long do Twitch VODs last before being deleted? +
Twitch stores past broadcasts based on account status: Regular streamers get 7 days. Twitch Affiliates get 14 days. Twitch Partners, Turbo subscribers, and Prime Gaming members get 60 days. After this retention window, Twitch permanently purges the video files from their servers.
2. Can I download a Twitch VOD for free? +
Yes! TwitchCutter is 100% free with no hidden charges, premium tiers, or download quotas. You can cut and download as many stream moments as you need.
3. How do I download a Twitch VOD before it expires? +
Simply copy the VOD link from Twitch before the expiration date, paste it into TwitchCutter, set your start and end timestamps, and hit Cut & Download Clip. Your MP4 will be saved safely on your device.
4. Can I download someone else's Twitch VOD? +
Yes. You can download segments from any public Twitch broadcast. Sub-only VODs require an active subscription on the streamer's channel and cannot be fetched anonymously.
5. What format are Twitch VODs downloaded in? +
All videos are downloaded in standard H.264 MP4 format with AAC audio, compatible across iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, and video editing software like DaVinci Resolve and Adobe Premiere.
6. Is there a limit to how long a VOD segment I can cut? +
No! While older tools like Untwitch cap downloads to 1-hour segments, TwitchCutter allows you to slice segments of any duration — from 10 seconds to several hours.
7. What happens to Twitch VODs after they expire? +
They are permanently erased from Twitch's CDN servers. Neither Twitch support nor third-party tools can recover a VOD once deleted, making early downloading essential.
8. Can I download Twitch VODs in 1080p? +
Yes! TwitchCutter automatically detects all stream qualities provided by the streamer and lets you download in full 1080p60 Source resolution.
9. Do Twitch highlights expire? +
No! Unlike standard past broadcasts, Twitch Highlights are stored permanently until the broadcaster manually deletes them.

✂️ Twitch Clips & Workarounds

10. How do I clip longer than 60 seconds on Twitch? +
Twitch's native clipping tool strictly enforces a 60-second limit. To bypass this, paste the full VOD link into TwitchCutter, set your start and end times to any duration (e.g. 5 minutes or 20 minutes), and download the extended clip as an MP4.
11. Do Twitch clips expire? +
No, Twitch clips do not have an automatic expiration timer. However, streamers can manually delete clips, or deleting the underlying VOD may sometimes render the clip unavailable. Downloading the MP4 gives you permanent offline ownership.
12. How do I download Twitch clips to my phone (iPhone & Android)? +
Visit twitchcutter.pages.dev on Safari or Chrome on your mobile device, paste the clip URL, and tap Download. You can save the MP4 directly into your Photos library or Files folder.
13. Can I download Twitch clips without a watermark? +
Yes! TwitchCutter adds zero watermarks, brand logos, or credit text. You get a clean, unbranded video export.
14. Is there a Twitch clip downloader with no signup required? +
Yes, TwitchCutter requires absolutely no signup, login, email, or Twitch account authorization.

16. Why is my Twitch VOD audio muted? +
Twitch automatically mutes sections containing copyrighted music (DMCA). The downloaded video will reflect these muted sections because the original audio was stripped at the source by Twitch.
