Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This policy outlines how TwitchCutter handles data when you use our service.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

TwitchCutter does not require registration, accounts, or payment credentials. We do not collect names, email addresses, or personal contact info.

2. Video Processing & Ephemeral Storage

When you submit a Twitch URL to slice a video segment, our server downloads only the specific video chunks required to assemble your clip. Generated MP4 files are stored in temporary memory and are automatically and permanently purged from server disks shortly after completion to prevent data retention.

3. Cookies & Tracking

TwitchCutter does not use tracking cookies or sell your browsing history to third-party data brokers.

4. External Links

Our website may reference external platforms such as Twitch.tv or GitHub. We encourage users to review the privacy practices of external platforms.
